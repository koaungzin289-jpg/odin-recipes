Shan Khauk Swe(Shan Noodle) Recipe

Description

Shan Khauk Swe is one of Myanmar's most famous and beloved noodle dishes, hailing from the Shan State in the northeastern highlands. Unlike soup-heavy noodles like Mohinga, this dish features thin, flat rice noodles tossed in a savory, oil-infused sauce made with minced chicken or pork, earthy fermented soybean paste (known as pè baung), and garlic oil. It can be prepared either as a rich dry noodle toss (dry style) or served with a light, flavorful chicken or pork broth on the side.

The dish is celebrated for its unique blend of textures and bold flavors. It is typically garnished with crushed roasted peanuts, toasted sesame seeds, crispy garlic, cilantro, and pickled mustard greens, offering a delightful combination of savory, nutty, and slightly tangy notes in every bite.

Ingredients

  • Flat rice noodles (Shan noodles) – 500 g
  • Ground chicken or pork – 250 g
  • Fermented soybean paste (Pè baung) – 2 tablespoons
  • Garlic – 6 cloves (finely minced)
  • Cooking oil – 4 tablespoons (or peanut oil)
  • Soy sauce – 1 tablespoon
  • Fish sauce – 1 tablespoon
  • Tomato paste – 1 tablespoon (optional, for a slight tang)
  • Salt and black pepper – to taste
  • Broth: Clear chicken or pork bone broth (simmered with garlic and white pepper)
  • Garnishes: Pickled mustard greens (mje-htauk), crushed roasted peanuts, toasted sesame seeds, crispy fried garlic, chopped cilantro, and chili oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the noodles: Boil the flat rice noodles in hot water according to package instructions until tender. Drain well and toss with a little oil to prevent sticking, then divide into serving bowls.
  2. Make the meat topping: Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the minced garlic until golden brown and fragrant, then remove half of it to use later as a crispy garnish.
  3. Cook the meat: In the remaining oil and garlic, add the ground chicken or pork. Stir-fry until the meat is lightly browned.
  4. Season the meat: Stir in the fermented soybean paste, soy sauce, fish sauce, and tomato paste. Add a splash of water, lower the heat, and simmer until the meat absorbs the rich, savory flavors and a fragrant oil separates on top.
  5. Assemble the dish: Spoon a generous portion of the seasoned meat sauce over the prepared noodles..
  6. Garnish and serve: Top with the pickled mustard greens, crushed roasted peanuts, toasted sesame seeds, the reserved crispy fried garlic, and fresh cilantro. Serve with a side bowl of hot, clear broth and a drizzle of chili oil.
